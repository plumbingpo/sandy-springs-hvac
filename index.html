Most air conditioner repairs in Sandy Springs run between $150 and $650, depending on the part. Common fixes like capacitors, contactors, and fan motors sit at the lower end, while compressor and refrigerant work costs more. If the system is beyond economical repair, full replacement typically ranges from $5,000 to $12,000 installed depending on size and efficiency. We diagnose first and give you a clear, upfront quote before any work begins.
A good rule of thumb: if the system is under 10 years old and the fix is a component, capacitor, motor, igniter, sensor, repair usually makes sense. If the compressor or heat exchanger has failed, the unit is past 12 to 15 years old, or repairs are stacking up season after season, replacement is the smarter investment. Our technicians walk you through both numbers honestly so you can decide with real information.
