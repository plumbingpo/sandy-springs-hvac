Full-Service HVAC Care for Savannah Homes and Businesses

Heating and cooling around here means a wide mix of equipment: older gas furnaces in established neighborhoods, high-efficiency heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team handles all of it. Our technicians have years in the field, and we've learned how the weather here, the age of local homes, and code requirements affect the way equipment performs over time.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or a seasonal tune-up. Repairs are usually straightforward: a failed capacitor, a dirty flame sensor, a refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling its way to an early failure. Maintenance visits cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, and nothing does more to extend equipment life.

Local businesses get the same care. A hot dining room or a freezing office drives customers and staff away, so our commercial crews focus on getting to the cause quickly and keep high-failure parts on board for rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment. Restaurants, retail shops, offices, and multi-unit buildings can count on us to schedule work around their business hours whenever we can.

When Your System Fails in Savannah

When your system quits, how quickly help arrives matters more than anything else. For that reason, we keep scheduling flexible across the area and put a real person on the phone. Emergency calls usually get a technician within a day, and we treat no-heat and no-cool calls as top priority.

The emergency calls we answer usually follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's warm air from the vents, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil frozen solid. On the heating side, it's a furnace that lights and then shuts down. Year-round, it's breakers that trip every cycle, plugged condensate drains overflowing into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have quit. Since our trucks are stocked with capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings, the majority of these calls are wrapped up on the first trip, at a price you approve before we start.

Two common situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can save you from a much larger repair.

How Savannah Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care is the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
