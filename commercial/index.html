Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Sandy Springs HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Sandy Springs

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling fast, throughout Sandy Springs and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. After that, we set up maintenance and monitoring to prevent a repeat, and every response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Sandy Springs HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes.
