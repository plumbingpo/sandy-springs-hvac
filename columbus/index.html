Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Columbus

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of summer.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a few miserable nights.
